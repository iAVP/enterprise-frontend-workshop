# Social Publication and Visual Identity Rules

## Language by platform

- **Hashnode:** publish full technical articles in English only.
- **LinkedIn:** publish announcements in German. Article/banner text may be English.
- **XING:** publish announcements in German, adapted as needed from LinkedIn.
- **VK:** publish Russian article versions and link to the English Hashnode article.

Do not publish Russian or German copies of articles on Hashnode.

## LinkedIn banner style

Use the accepted EXXETA-inspired banner featuring the “The Road to Architecture” composition as the visual reference for the Enterprise Frontend Workshop series.

- Follow the EXXETA corporate-inspired visual language: white/light background, dark navy typography, yellow/gold accents, and clean geometric overlays.
- Include the EXXETA name/logo in its proper form when appropriate and retain the series name **ENTERPRISE FRONTEND WORKSHOP**.
- Use a wide horizontal banner with the title and a short subtitle on the left, and a relevant technical illustration, architecture diagram, or thematic image on the right.
- Keep the design professional, readable, restrained, and consistent across articles.
- Avoid the dark neon/tech style with oversized NX and Angular logos as the default LinkedIn banner style.
- Use the approved “The Road to Architecture” banner as a style reference, not as a requirement to reuse its road image for every topic.
- Do not imply that a personal post or banner is an official EXXETA corporate publication. Do not invent, distort, or redraw the EXXETA logo.

## LinkedIn post format

- Write the post body in German.
- Keep the tone professional and personal, without grandiosity or claiming to speak on behalf of EXXETA.
- Include the direct link to the full English Hashnode article.
- Add relevant hashtags. Prefer a focused set such as `#SoftwareArchitecture`, `#Angular`, `#NX`, `#Monorepo`, `#FrontendDevelopment`, `#TypeScript`, `#DDD`, and `#EnterpriseFrontend`; select only those relevant to the specific article.

## Article publication workflow

1. Prepare and review the full English article.
2. Create a Hashnode draft, set title, cover, tags, and SEO description, and check the preview before publishing.
3. After publication, use its canonical URL in German LinkedIn and XING announcements and in the Russian VK post.
4. Keep source-language versions in the repository; publish them only on the platform assigned to that language above.
