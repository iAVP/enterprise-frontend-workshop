# Article Subtitles

This file is the canonical source for the subtitles of the seven published articles in the Enterprise Frontend Workshop.

## Editorial positioning

The workshop is about enterprise frontend engineering and, above all, the architectural thinking behind it. Frameworks, libraries, IDEs, and tools are implementation choices and examples—not the overall subject of the series.

Readers should be able to recognize this positioning before opening an article. Each Hashnode publication should therefore use its subtitle to make the architectural and engineering perspective explicit, especially when the title names a specific technology.

Use the subtitles below as the approved wording for the existing publications. Keep article titles and article body text unchanged unless a separate editorial task explicitly requests otherwise.

## Rule for future publications

Every new article must have a subtitle that:

- identifies the publication as part of the Enterprise Frontend Workshop;
- communicates the engineering or architectural value, not merely the technology name;
- accurately reflects the actual article content;
- uses relevant technical terms naturally without turning the subtitle into a keyword list.

The technology may lead the title, but the subtitle should reveal the broader architectural question or engineering problem addressed by the article.
